# Concept 02: The Yard

> Generated 2026-04-09 by 7-agent creative team (round 2) + Creative Director synthesis.
> Status: CANDIDATE (not selected — comparison pending)

## Core Identity
The Jailyard Dynasty isn't a publication you read — it's a surveillance operation you're running. Every week, you receive a classified briefing on 12 inmates competing for yard supremacy, built from intercepted comms, behavioral analysis, and field intelligence. The site doesn't explain what happened. It makes you feel like you already know too much.

## Five Signature Features
1. **The Count** — Cell block tier assignments (Penthouse / A / B / C / The Hole). Teams animate between blocks. Each block has distinct visual degradation.
2. **The Polygraph** — Weekly behavioral forensics. Cross-references what owners SAY vs. what they DO. Computes Reputation Archetypes (Shark, Diplomat, Hermit, Arsonist, Mark).
3. **The Drop** — Synchronized Tuesday 9 PM reveal. Countdown timer. Rankings declassify simultaneously. Group chat explodes during the 4-minute window.
4. **The Grudge Ledger** — AI writer with permanent cross-season memory. References 2023 failures in 2025 columns. Never forgets.
5. **Reputation Archetypes** — Computed behavioral classifications that evolve across the season. Visual badges. Shift events are automatically narrated.

## Design Language
- Near-black (#0a0a0a), fluorescent green (#39ff14), amber warnings, red threats
- Monospace data text (JetBrains Mono) + condensed sans-serif headlines (Barlow Condensed)
- CRT scan lines, concrete grain, redaction bars, stamp marks (CLASSIFIED, EVICTED)
- Dense, information-forward. No hero images, no whitespace breathing room.
- Motion: typewriter reveals, threat level pulses, rank transitions. No shimmer/particle effects.

## Season Arc
- Weeks 1-4: INTAKE (establishing baselines, prior convictions carry forward)
- Weeks 5-9: YARD POLITICS (Heat Index, Power Map, alliances visible)
- Weeks 10-13: LOCKDOWN (Eviction Notices, heightened surveillance)
- Week 14: THE TRIBUNAL (league votes on 3 cases, rulings become case law)
- Weeks 15-17: THE YARD playoffs (elimination bracket, winner-stays)
- Championship: THE VERDICT (dossier for champion, autopsy for loser)

## Weekly Experience
Tuesday 9PM: The Drop (synchronized countdown reveal)
Tuesday-Thursday: Briefing (SitRep, The Count, Intercepted Comms, Polygraph, Informant Tips)
Friday: Match Orders (upcoming matchup threat assessments)
Sunday-Monday: After-Action Report (results processed as field intelligence)

## Shareability Engine
- Dossier cards per team (block assignment, threat level, analyst note, archetype badge)
- Polygraph result cards (what they said vs. what they did)
- Eviction Notices (full-page mock-official documents)
- Tribunal rulings as case law citations

## History Experience
"The Permanent Record" — archived case files per season. Elo as "Threat Assessment Scores." H2H as "Known Engagements." Championships as "Convicted Champions" wall. Cross-season archetype evolution tracking.

## Provocative Elements
- YES: Public indexing (Googleable), Stakes via Tribunal disciplinary actions, Grudge Ledger
- NO: Kangaroo Court (muddies voice), Expansion Draft (dilutes focus), AI Podcast (breaks conceit)

## What We're NOT Building
No magazine/publication aesthetic. No serif typography. No data dashboards. No interactive viz requiring complex Canvas. No backend/auth. No mobile app.

## Licensing Hook
"Every fantasy league has a group chat. No one has a surveillance operation." The Count, Polygraph, Drop, Grudge Ledger, and Archetypes are all league-agnostic. Swap the data source and any dynasty league becomes a Yard.
