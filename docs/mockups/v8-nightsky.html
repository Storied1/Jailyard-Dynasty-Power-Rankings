Kittler on the Roof held the number one ranking for exactly seven days. One week. That’s how long the defending champion sat on the throne before MHJTIME walked in and dropped 140.94 on the crown.
Rico Dowdle went for 30.9. Ja’Marr Chase added 20.1. Jayden Daniels orchestrated with 22.64. And Kittler’s 107.74 sent the defending champion tumbling from first to fifth in the most dramatic single-week fall the power rankings have recorded.
Last week, five teams shared a penthouse at 4-1 and we called it unprecedented parity. This week, three of them separated at 5-1, and both of the other two dropped out of the top four entirely.
The Legion of Bouz reclaimed the number one spot with 165.9 — Mahomes and Cam Skattebo each eclipsed 30 points — while General Ken-obi stormed from fifth to second behind a 164.94 demolition that featured the week’s most absurd stat line: the Rams defense scored 27.0 points.
The group chat turned into a trade bazaar. Brent opened the market with a casual “Hit me up if in need of.” Karim countered with “2/3 top QBs come at me.” And then Ben delivered the line of the week: “If you want some old serviceable WR3/4s with championship pedigree instead of Zach’s 2nd place also rans, let me know.”
Circle next week in permanent marker: The Legion of Bouz versus General Ken-obi. First versus second. Both 5-1. Two former champions with three titles between them. Mahomes versus Maye. The Jailyard doesn’t do regular-season Super Bowls — until it does.
