WEEK 9
November 3, 2025
Scroll

There are weeks in a dynasty season where the standings shift and you feel it coming, like tectonic plates grinding underneath the surface. Week 9 was not one of those weeks. Week 9 was the earthquake. The kind that rearranges furniture and sends owners spiraling into group chat meltdowns at 1 AM, arguing about whether Puka Nacua is a league-winner or a glorified decoy. This was the week that separated pretenders from contenders, and brother, the pretenders got absolutely demolished.

Five of the top six teams lost this week. Read that again. Five of six. The entire top half of the power rankings looked at Week 9 and collectively decided to take a vacation. Meanwhile, the teams everyone had written off? They came in swinging like they had nothing to lose, because they didn't. The chaos index hit a season-high, and the group chat nearly imploded trying to process all of it in real time.

142.7
The highest-scoring loss in league history. Sometimes the fantasy gods just want to watch you suffer.

General Ken-obi posted 142.7 points and still lost. That's not a typo. That's a war crime. In any other week in any other league, 142 gets you a trophy, a parade, and maybe a key to the city. In the Jailyard this week, it got you an L and a very expensive therapy bill. The man did everything right, his roster exploded for numbers we haven't seen since Week 3, and it still wasn't enough because his opponent decided to have the game of his life at the exact worst possible moment. Dynasty is a cruel, beautiful sport, and Week 9 is Exhibit A in the prosecution's case.

Dynasty is the only sport where you can do everything right and still wake up on Monday morning wondering what the point of it all was. The Jailyard Group Chat, 1:47 AM

Power Rankings

1
#1 7-2 —

General Ken-obi

You dropped 142.7 points and still lost, which is either the unluckiest thing that's ever happened in this league or definitive proof that the simulation is rigged against you personally. Here's the thing though: you're still sitting at 7-2 with the second-highest points-for in the league. Your roster is a machine. Lamar is playing like he wants a third MVP. Puka is finally healthy and producing WR1 numbers. The loss stings, but you're the team nobody wants to see in the playoffs. Take the L, pour something expensive, and remember: 142 points doesn't lie. You're the best team in this league and everyone knows it.

#5 5-4 ↓2

Legion of Bouz

Remember when you were 4-0 and the group chat was crowning you the runaway favorite? That was five weeks and four losses ago. You've gone 1-4 since the bye, and the vibes have shifted from "unbeatable juggernaut" to "team that peaked in September." Your ceiling is still elite when everyone's healthy and firing, but your floor has been horrifying. CeeDee Lamb is giving you WR2 numbers at best, your RB depth is a prayer and a bus ticket, and you're leaking points at a rate that should concern anyone with a playoff bracket circled on their calendar. The talent is there. The consistency isn't. Fix it before the Jailyard forgets you were ever on top.

8
#8 4-5 ↑3

Chudders Football Team

You're the Week 9 chaos agent and you're not apologizing for it. A three-game win streak has dragged you from the basement back into the playoff conversation, and the scary part is your roster actually looks like it's figured something out. Your waiver-wire pickups have been surgical, your lineup decisions have been sharp, and your points-against luck has finally started regressing to the mean. You're still a game under .500, which means nobody's scared of you yet, but that's exactly how you want it. The league's sleeping on Chudders. Keep it that way.

#12 2-7 ↓1

Father Time

What is there left to say? You're 2-7. Your roster is aging like milk in the sun. Your best player is a running back on a team that doesn't want to run the ball, your QB situation is a revolving door of disappointment, and your dynasty assets have the trade value of a used napkin. The group chat has officially started referring to your matchup as "the bye week," which is brutal but also statistically accurate since your opponents are averaging 128 points against you. The rebuild isn't coming, it's here. Sell everything. Acquire picks. Accept the tank. Father Time waits for no one, but ironically, Father Time's team is going to be waiting a very long time for relevance.

Mailbag

Is it time to panic about the Legion? Four losses in five weeks feels like more than a slump.
Kevin, via group chat

I'm not hitting the panic button yet, but I'm definitely standing next to it with my hand hovering. The talent is real. CeeDee, Breece, the stack with Dak -- on paper, this is a top-three roster. But paper doesn't win matchups, and the Legion has been getting outcoached at the lineup level for a month straight. The wrong guys are in the flex. The streamers aren't streaming. And the schedule isn't getting easier. I'll say this: if the Legion goes 1-2 in the next three weeks, we're not talking about a slump anymore. We're talking about a full-blown identity crisis. The window is closing, and the playoff picture doesn't care about your September record.

Which team scares you most going into the second half of the season?
Marcus, via DM

Chudders. And I know that sounds insane given they're 4-5, but hear me out. They've won three straight. Their roster is clicking at the right time. And most importantly, nobody is gameplanning for them. Every other contender is looking at Ken-obi or the Legion when they think about playoff matchups. Nobody's looking over their shoulder at the 4-5 team with momentum and nothing to lose. That's the most dangerous team in any fantasy league -- the one that gets hot late, sneaks into the bracket, and suddenly has the swagger of a team that's been there all year. If Chudders makes the playoffs, I don't want to see them in Round 1.
