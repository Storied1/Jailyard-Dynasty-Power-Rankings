The Week
Ken-obi
Went Nuclear

There is a moment in every fantasy season where the standings stop being hypothetical and start becoming fate. General Ken-obi just made that moment Week 9. 154.86 points. A 62.48-point margin of victory over Kittler on the Roof, who managed a heartbreaking 92.38. Drake London went supernova: 34.3 points. LaPorta added 18.7. The defending champ didn't just lose this week — the defending champ got exposed.

Meanwhile, MHJTIME quietly stacked another win, 144.48 to The Boonist Monks' 130.36. That's five straight. Rico Dowdle — a running back nobody wanted in August — dropped 27.1 points and looked like the waiver wire steal of the season. Marvin Harrison added 19.1. The WR trio is doing exactly what everyone feared it would.

Rasheeing the Scene pulled the week's most satisfying upset, knocking off The Legion of Bouz 146.92 to 135.2. Josh Allen did Josh Allen things — 28.82 points — and Legion's slide continues. Two straight losses. A drop from first to fifth. CMC's 31.8 wasn't enough. Mahomes' 11.5 was the quiet disaster.

At the bottom, things are getting ugly. Chudders Football Team sits at 1-8. Ghastly Grayskull Gang has lost five in a row. Burden of Etienne-y Woody finally won one — by 2.14 points over Noble FFT — and celebrated like it was the Super Bowl. When you're 2-7, a win IS the Super Bowl.

The standings have split into two realities. Three teams at 7-2. Two at 6-3. Then a cliff. The separation is happening right in front of us, and it's not subtle. Sleeping Giants escaped Grayskull by just 2.04 points in the week's closest game. Brock Bowers had 37.3 in a loss. Fantasy football remains a game where the best individual performance doesn't guarantee anything.

Father Time beat Chudders by 16.62 to snap a brutal skid. It doesn't fix the 2-7 record. It doesn't change the trajectory. But Davante Adams dropping 20.5 and the Ravens D posting 19.0 at least gave them something to talk about in a week where most of the bottom six had nothing.
