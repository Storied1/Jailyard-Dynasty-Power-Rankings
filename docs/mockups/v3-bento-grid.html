The Column

The Unkillable Team at the Top and the Empire Crumbling Below

There are weeks that reorder your priors, and then there is Week 9 in the Jailyard. Ken-obi dropped 165 on a roster that had no business putting up 165. Lamar sat on the bench. The "wrong" lineup still obliterated the field. That is what happens when a team is operating on a different frequency than the rest of the league — the mistakes don't even matter because the margin is that obscene. Seven wins in nine weeks. The point-scoring machine doesn't have an off switch, and the rest of you are still looking for the plug.

Meanwhile, the bottom half of the standings has turned into a telenovela. Father Time is running a roster that looks like it was assembled during a power outage — 2-7 and somehow still finding new ways to lose. The trade deadline is approaching and half the league is trying to figure out whether to buy, sell, or just close the app and pretend the season isn't happening. The group chat has been unhinged.

Q: Is it time to panic about Legion of Bouz? They were 4-0 and now they look lost. — @RealBouzFan
Not panic. But it's time to stop pretending the 4-0 start was a statement. It was a schedule gift. The Legion played four bottom-half teams in the first month and beat them in ways that looked impressive until you realized the competition was running 2019 rosters. Now the schedule has teeth, and the roster has questions — specifically at receiver, where "WR2 by committee" is not a strategy so much as an admission of defeat. The next three weeks are all playoff-caliber opponents. If they go 1-2 or worse, the narrative flips from "early frontrunner" to "cautionary tale" faster than you can say "sell high on your aging RB1."
