There are decisions in fantasy football that haunt you for a week, and then there are decisions that follow you into the group chat afterlife. Goff finished with 11.2 points. Lamar went for 34.8 on the bench. The math isn’t hard. The emotional damage is permanent.

What makes Week 9 different from your average mid-season shrug is the sheer volume of receipts. General Ken-obi’s roster posted 165.4 points—the highest single-week total in the league this year—and did it without a single player going nuclear. Just seven guys all clearing 15 points like it was coordinated. Meanwhile, the bottom of the standings discovered new ways to lose, and the group chat couldn’t look away.

The championship isn’t locked, but the odds board is tilting hard.
“Father Time benched Lamar for Goff. Does this qualify as the worst start/sit decision of the season?”
@bLaker24
It’s the worst one so far—and that “so far” is doing a lot of heavy lifting because the bar was already high. Lamar had put up 30+ in three straight weeks. Goff was facing a Packers defense that had allowed the fourth-fewest passing yards since Week 4. The data was screaming. Sometimes you just have to trust the guy who has been literally the best fantasy quarterback alive for a month straight. This one is going in the group chat Hall of Fame.
