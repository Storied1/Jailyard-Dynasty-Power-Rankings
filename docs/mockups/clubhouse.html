Week 9 / The Jailyard Dynasty / Nov 5, 2025

He really started Goff over Lamar.

The group chat went silent for eleven minutes. That's longer than any of you were quiet during the draft. Then someone just typed "bro." and the floodgates opened.

The Column

There are weeks where the fantasy gods reach into the wire, yank out an outcome so absurd that no amount of optimization or roster-tinkering could have predicted it, and drop it in your lap like a cat leaving a dead bird on the doorstep. Week 9 was that cat. Three of the top four scorers were on bye. The league's most-traded player this season scored 42 points on someone's bench. And General Ken-obi, who has been operating with the quiet menace of a card counter at a casino that hasn't caught on yet, put together a 168-point masterpiece against a Legion team that genuinely thought this was their week to make a statement.

This league has a funny way of punishing confidence. You don't get smoked when you expect to lose. You get smoked the week you finally tell the group chat you feel good about your lineup.

The broader picture is starting to crystallize in a way that should terrify at least four of you. We're past the halfway point now, and the gap between the playoff teams and the pretenders isn't shrinking -- it's calcifying. The teams at the top have depth. The teams at the bottom have hope, and hope is the most dangerous drug in dynasty because it convinces you to hold players you should be selling and sell futures you're going to need. Father Time is 2-7. That's not a rough patch. That's a franchise in freefall, and the trade deadline is two weeks away.

But here's the thing about dynasty that makes it unlike anything else in fantasy sports: the 2-7 team isn't dead. They're just playing a different game now. And if they play it right -- if they sell the aging assets, stockpile the picks, and trust the process -- they'll be the team everyone's afraid of in 2027. The question is whether they have the stomach for it.

At 5-4 you're still solidly in the playoff picture, but the margin for error just got thinner than your RB depth. Stop overthinking your lineup. The answer is usually the obvious one.

12 Father Time

The rebuild starts now. Or it doesn't, and that's worse.

Your roster is old, your picks are gone, and your best player is quietly asking to be traded in the DMs. There are two moves here: sell everything that isn't nailed down and stockpile 2026 picks, or keep running it back and finish 3-14. One of those paths leads somewhere. Choose wisely.
