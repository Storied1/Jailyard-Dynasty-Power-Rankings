Chapter VI

OF THE GREAT UPSETS, AND THE FALL OF THE DEFENDING CHAMPION
❦ ❧ ❦

In the sixth week of the year of our Lord Two Thousand and Twenty Five, the Houses did gather, and what a week it was, for four of the six contests did end in upsettum most grievous. The defending champion, bchodos of Kittler on the Roof, who had but a week prior sat atop the table as Number the First, did fall to rango_ of MHJTIME by the score of one hundred forty-and-ninety-four hundredths to one hundred seven-and-seventy-four. Thus did Kittler tumble unto the fifth seat, his W become an L, his crown set askew.

Meanwhile, at the top of the realm, the twin banners of The Legion of Bouz and General Ken-obi did march in lockstep, each scoring in the high one-and-sixty region, and each laying waste to the lowly 0-and-6 Houses of the cellar. KidBouzie's Legion did put unto the sword the House of Father Time, 165.90 unto 107.66, for the week's highest tally. The good bLaker24, whose General Ken-obi now doth bear an Elo of seventeen hundred — a peak not before witnessed in his career — did lay low the Burden of Etienne-y Woody by sixty-and-five points, the biggest blowout of the week.

In the House of Noble, the son kevobucks did summon the spirit of Bijan Robinson, who didst run for thirty-and-two-tenths scoring runs, a feat not seen since the lost age. By such grace did Noble FFT, thought but a 2-and-3 middling sort, put the Ghastly Grayskull Gang unto the earth, 151.88 unto 121.30, an upsettum of thirty-and-a-half points.

The Boonist Monks of GauchoTrain, guided by the hand of George Pickens (27.3) and Jaxon Smith-Njigba (26.2), did deliver unto Rasheeing the Scene a lesson most humbling, 150.46 unto 109.10 — an upsettum by the margin of forty-and-one points. Even the Sleeping Giants of zbcowan, sometimes sluggish yet ever plodding, did extend their winning streak unto W-the-fourth, quelling Chudders FT 129.82 unto 95.26 upon the shoulders of Josh Jacobs (29.5).

Now the table is wrought in fire: three Houses tied at 5-and-1 — the Legion, Ken-obi, and the Sleeping Giants — with the Monks nipping at their heels at 4-and-2. Yea, and the cellar doth groan, for both kharlow's Burden and Redrumsregrub's Father Time are 0-and-6, their losing streaks stretching unto six long weeks. The group chat, as ever the truest chronicler, hath spoken, and it hath spoken loudly. Gods preserve the Vessels of Kittler.
