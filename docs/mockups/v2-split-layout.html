Week 9

Three Teams at 7-2 and Nobody Knows Who's Real

Ken-obi drops 154 on the defending champ, MHJTIME's five-game heater quietly rewrites the season, and the Legion falls from first to fifth in two weeks.

Something broke in this league around Week 7, and I'm not sure we've figured out how to put it back together. Two weeks ago The Legion of Bouz was sitting at 6-1, Mahomes was doing Mahomes things, and the rest of us were arguing about who'd be lucky enough to face them in the semifinals rather than the finals. Then they lost. Then they lost again. Now KidBouzie is 6-3, dropping from first to fifth in the power rankings like a guy who showed up to a black-tie event in cargo shorts. Christian McCaffrey went for 31.8 this week and it didn't matter because Rasheeing the Scene had Josh Allen going full video game. The 146.92 from ToreroGaucho felt almost casual, the kind of score that says "I've been here the whole time, you just weren't paying attention."

Meanwhile, General Ken-obi decided to remind Kittler on the Roof what it feels like to play a real team. A 62.48-point margin. Drake London put up 34.3 points like he was personally offended by the defending champion's existence. Kittler scored 92.38, their lowest total since... actually, let's not look that up. The 2024 champs have now lost four straight and dropped to 4-5. The floor didn't just crack; it fell through into the basement. You hate to see a dynasty defending its title with a kicker outscoring their starting running back, but here we are.

But the real story of Week 9 — and maybe the real story of the entire first half — is MHJTIME's silent five-game winning streak. No one talks about rango_ because talking about rango_ means admitting you were wrong about the running backs. Rico Dowdle dropped 27.1. Marvin Harrison Jr. went for 19.1. Even Daniel Jones — Daniel Jones! — contributed a serviceable 17 from the quarterback spot. They've climbed from the bottom half to 6-3 and second in the power rankings, and the scariest part is that Ja'Marr Chase and Malik Nabers haven't even had their monster weeks yet. When both of them go off in the same Sunday? Pray for whoever's on the other side of that matchup.

Mailbag
@GrayskullXX via group chat "Is Kittler on the Roof cooked? Four straight losses and Brock Bowers just hung 37.3 on them in a loss. What's happening?"

They're not cooked — they're medium rare and getting colder by the week. The 2024 champ is now 4-5 with the league's lowest score this week (92.38). Jonathan Taylor and Tony Pollard have been ghosts. Joe Burrow isn't broken, but the supporting cast is crumbling around him like a sandcastle at high tide. The good news: the schedule eases up. The bad news: you don't get style points for "easing up" when your season is on the line. They need to win four of their last five to feel safe, and right now nothing about this roster feels safe.

@Chudders via DM "I'm 1-8 and my only win was in Week 3. Am I allowed to start talking about the 2026 draft yet?"

Brother, you've been allowed since Week 5. You have the league's richest draft capital — multiple firsts in both 2026 and 2027. Justin Herbert is 27. Jahmyr Gibbs and Ashton Jeanty are going to be monsters. The 2025 season is a write-off and everyone knows it, including you, including the group chat that roasts you every Sunday at 4 PM. But here's the thing: the teams you're losing to now are the ones who'll be selling you their future picks in two months. Patience isn't glamorous, but it's the only play.
