There's a moment in every dynasty season when the noise stops and the signal comes through clean. Week 9 was that moment. General Ken-obi didn't just win — they detonated Kittler on the Roof by 62.48 points, the kind of margin that makes you check if the other team actually set a lineup. Drake London went supernova with 34.3 points, and suddenly the preseason #1 pick looked like exactly what everyone feared they'd become: inevitable.

Meanwhile, The Legion of Bouz is learning what happens when you build a dynasty on vibes and McCaffrey alone. Two straight losses — including getting handled by Rasheeing the Scene, who apparently decided that Josh Allen + Rashee Rice is a personality now. The Legion dropped from first to fifth in a single week. That's not a stumble. That's the league telling you the throne doesn't come with a warranty.

Three teams at 7-2. One team at 1-8. The middle is a knife fight and the bottom is a graveyard.

The real story is MHJTIME riding a five-game win streak with Rico Dowdle — yes, Rico Dowdle — leading the charge with 27.1 points. When your RB1 is a waiver-wire pickup outscoring Bijan Robinson, you're either a genius or you're living on borrowed time. Either way, 6-3 with the league's highest point total (1,411.5) means this team is dangerously real. The chat knows it. The standings confirm it. The rest of you should be worried.

Power Rankings

1 General Ken-obi 7-2 · 1,348.0 PF

The 62-point margin over Kittler wasn't a game — it was a statement of fact. Drake London, Sam LaPorta, and Chase Brown all cleared 16 points. When your floor looks like everyone else's ceiling, you're not competing. You're just waiting for the bracket to catch up.

2 MHJTIME 6-3 · 1,411.5 PF

Five straight wins and the league's highest point total. Rico Dowdle leading the RB room is either an indictment or a miracle. Marvin Harrison Jr. and Ja'Marr Chase as your WR combo is borderline unfair. The only question: can Daniel Jones really be your quarterback in December?

3 Rasheeing the Scene 7-2 · 1,278.8 PF

Three straight wins including taking down the Legion. Josh Allen dropped 28.82 and this team has quietly become the most balanced roster in the league. De'Von Achane and Travis Etienne provide the floor. Rashee Rice provides the ceiling. And you're providing nightmares for whoever draws you in the playoffs.

4 Sleeping Giants 7-2 · 1,280.2 PF

Beat Grayskull by 2.04 points — the week's closest game. Sam Darnold leading your scoring feels like a punchline, but 7-2 is 7-2. The name has never been more apt: a roster of aging stars who refuse to acknowledge the calendar. Josh Jacobs and Derrick Henry keep grinding. The clock keeps ticking.
