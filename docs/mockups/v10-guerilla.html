The Breakdown

Chaos doesn't knock. Four of six matchups ended in upsets, the defending champion cratered from first to fifth, and two teams at the top of the standings put up nearly identical 165-point explosions on the same afternoon. The Legion of Bouz rode Patrick Mahomes (30.48) and Cam Skattebo (30.0) to the week's highest score. Meanwhile, General Ken-obi's LAR DEF (27.0) and Drake London (26.8) quietly assembled an equally devastating 164.94. Both sit at 5-1 now. Both collide next week.

Kittler on the Roof's 107.74 was a season low for a team that entered the week ranked first. MHJTIME's Rico Dowdle (30.9) and Ja'Marr Chase (20.1) gutted them. Down in the cellar, Burden and Father Time both locked in their sixth straight loss. They play each other next week in what can only be called the Toilet Bowl Matinee — someone's winless streak ends, but nobody wins.

The real headline: Bijan Robinson's 32.8-point eruption dragged Noble FFT to a 30-point upset win. The man is carrying that franchise on his deltoids. League average this week: 128.7. Only four teams cleared it. The standings shuffled. The vibes shifted. And next week, #1 meets #2.
