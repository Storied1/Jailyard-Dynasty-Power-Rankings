Jailyard Dynasty — Case File #006

The Week the Crown Fell

A detective's account of Week 6 in the Jailyard.

I'd seen bad weeks before. I'd seen teams collapse like cheap suits in a rainstorm. But Week 6 — Week 6 was something else entirely.

Four upsets out of six games. The defending champion, Kittler on the Roof, put up a measly 107.74 points and got dragged from the top of the standings down to number five. Fell from #1 to #5 like a body off a fire escape. Meanwhile two squads — Legion of Bouz and General Ken-obi — both lit up the scoreboard north of 164, both sitting pretty at 5-1, on a collision course for Week 7. The kind of matchup that makes the whole league hold its breath.

The league average was 128.7 points. A quiet number. But averages lie. This was a week of extremes — of 165-point eruptions and 95-point flatlines. The kind of week that separates the guilty from the merely suspicious.

I poured myself another drink and opened the case file.
