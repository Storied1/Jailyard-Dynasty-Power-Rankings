WEEK 9 The Column

There is a specific kind of panic that sets in around Week 9 of a dynasty season, and it has nothing to do with your actual roster. It is the slow, creeping realization that the narratives you built in August -- the ones you repeated to yourself in the mirror while brushing your teeth, the ones you whispered into the group chat at 1 a.m. like a football shaman -- are colliding with the cold math of a standings page that could not care less about your feelings.

General Ken-obi sits at 7-2 and the rest of the league is doing that thing where they pretend not to notice. Nobody wants to crown anyone in Week 9. That is a jinx that transcends sport. But here is the thing about Ken-obi: the roster is not just winning. It is winning the way a team wins when the infrastructure is sound. Points for is first, points against is third-lowest, and the schedule has been moderately difficult. This is not a house of cards. This is a house of bricks with a moat and a guy checking IDs at the door.

62.5
The margin in the week's biggest blowout. Father Time did not just lose -- they got eulogized.

Meanwhile, the middle of the standings looks like a mosh pit at a concert where nobody knows the band. Five teams at 5-4, all of them convinced they are one waiver wire pickup away from a championship run. Spoiler: at least three of them are wrong, and the other two are going to need injury luck that borders on divine intervention. The playoff cutoff line at rank 6 is going to be a bloodbath over the final four weeks, and the group chat already smells like desperation.

Five teams at 5-4, all of them convinced they are one move away from a title run. At least three of them are lying to themselves.

Mailbag

Is it too early to start tanking if you are 3-6? Asking for a friend whose team name rhymes with "Schmoonist Schmonks."

-- Anonymous (definitely not the Boonist Monks owner)

Look, I am not going to sit here and tell you that 3-6 is a death sentence in a 12-team league with 6 playoff spots. Mathematically, you are alive. But you are alive the way a houseplant is alive when you have forgotten to water it for three weeks and there is one green leaf hanging on. The question is not "can you make the playoffs" -- it is "should you be allocating your emotional bandwidth to a 12% chance when you could be building for 2026?" The answer depends on how much you enjoy suffering.

Father Time is 2-7. At what point does the league intervene? Like a wellness check, but for fantasy football.

-- Concerned League Citizen

Father Time is having the kind of season that makes you rethink the entire concept of dynasty football. Last in points for, last in points against differential, and the schedule is not getting any easier. This is not a team that is unlucky. This is a team that is methodically speedrunning the worst season in league history. The only intervention available is the draft lottery, and honestly, that might be the plan. Respect the long game, even when the long game looks like a controlled demolition.
