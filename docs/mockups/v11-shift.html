Jailyard Dynasty · Season 2025 · Week 6

The Week That Changed Everything

Three teams sat at 5-1 entering Sunday. The defending champion held the top spot. The models had their predictions. The standings looked stable, settled, almost boring in their predictability. It was, by every measure, supposed to be a routine week in the Jailyard Dynasty.

It was not a routine week.

The Setup

Kittler on the Roof — the 2024 champion, runner-up in 2022 and 2023 — sat at #1 in the power rankings with a 4-1 record. Below them, a traffic jam: Legion of Bouz, Sleeping Giants, Rasheeing the Scene, and General Ken-obi all at 4-1. The Boonist Monks lurked at 3-2 with the league's highest points-for.

At the bottom, Burden of Etienne-y Woody and Father Time both sat at 0-5, sinking toward the toilet bowl with a combined record of zero wins in ten games.

The standings looked stable.
Almost boring.

Then Sunday happened.

The First Crack

Noble FFT Ghastly Grayskull Gang
151.88 — 121.30
Bijan Robinson: 32.8 pts · Caleb Williams: 19.88
Upset · +30.58

Bijan Robinson put up 32.8 points — the highest individual score of the week. Noble FFT had been sitting at 2-3, mired in the middle of the pack. Ghastly Grayskull had the better record, the better roster on paper, the better odds. None of it mattered when Robinson decided to run through the entire Atlanta schedule.

The Dethroning

MHJTIME Kittler on the Roof
140.94 — 107.74
Rico Dowdle: 30.9 · Jayden Daniels: 22.64 · Ja'Marr Chase: 20.1
Upset · +33.20

This is the one that rewrote the standings. Kittler — the defending champion, the team that had been #1 all season — scored 107.74. Below the league average of 128.7. Against a MHJTIME squad whose trio of Dowdle, Daniels, and Chase combined for 73.64 points. Three players. Nearly seventy-four points.

The throne isn't just empty.
It's in pieces.

The Avalanche

The Boonist Monks Rasheeing the Scene
150.46 — 109.10
George Pickens: 27.3 · Jaxon Smith-Njigba: 26.2 · Flacco: 18.76
Upset · +41.36

Pickens and Smith-Njigba combined for 53.5 from the wide receiver room alone. Rasheeing the Scene's Josh Allen — normally a cheat code — managed only 15.4. The Boonist Monks climbed two spots. Rasheeing fell two. The whole middle of the standings liquefied.

The Blowout

General Ken-obi Burden of Etienne-y Woody
164.94 — 99.20
LA Rams DEF: 27.0 · Drake London: 26.8 · Drake Maye: 26.24
Blowout · +65.74

Sixty-five points. The largest margin of the entire 2025 season. The Rams defense — a defense! — scored 27.0 points by themselves, more than Burden's top individual performer. Ken-obi's Elo climbed to 1700.2. A career peak. Two-time champion. Five-and-one. The trajectory says: title contender.

Burden fell to 0-6. Their sixth consecutive loss. Next week they play Father Time — also 0-6. Someone gets their first win. Someone extends the misery.

Four of six games.
Upsets.

The New Order

When the dust settled, three teams shared 5-1 records at the top. One of them — Ken-obi — had risen from 5th to 2nd. One — Kittler — had dropped from 1st to 5th. The entire top half of the standings reshuffled.

What Comes Next

Week 7 delivers the matchup the season has been building toward: The Legion of Bouz vs General Ken-obi. #1 vs #2. Both 5-1. Both scored 164+ this week. Elo: 1604 vs 1700. One former champion vs a two-time champion. One of them walks out of Week 7 with sole possession of first place.

At the other end: Burden of Etienne-y Woody vs Father Time. 0-6 vs 0-6. Someone's losing streak ends. Someone's doesn't. The toilet bowl matinee nobody asked for but everyone will watch.

The models broke this week.
