Week 9 / The Jailyard

The Landscape

Twelve teams. Four quadrants. Nine weeks of evidence. The scatter plot you're standing inside tells you everything the standings won't: who's dominant, who's cursed, who's been getting away with murder, and who never had a chance.

X-axis is points scored. Y-axis is points allowed. The bigger the circle, the more wins. Find your team. Then find the truth.

The quadrant doesn't lie. The record sometimes does.

Top-Right Quadrant

The Dominant

Ken-obi lives in the penthouse of this chart — outscoring everyone while giving up the fewest points. That's not luck. That's structural superiority. At 7-2, the only thing separating them from a perfect record is two weeks where the fantasy gods decided to make a point.

MHJTIME is right there too — the points for are real, the defense is stiff, and the 6-3 record undersells how good this roster is week to week.

This is where championships are built. Not in the standings — in the margins.

Top-Left Quadrant

The Cursed

Kittler is putting up 1,220 points through nine weeks and somehow staring at a 5-4 record. That's because you're also giving up 1,200. You're in a shootout every single week and nobody told you to bring a helmet.

Rasheeing is flirting with this quadrant too — 1,250 PF against 1,120 PA. The offense is there. The problem is the universe keeps matching you against other people's best weeks.

The Cursed don't lose because they're bad. They lose because they're interesting.

Bottom-Left Quadrant

The Frauds

Chudders at 4-5 with 1,100 PF and 1,050 PA. You're not winning games. You're surviving them. The points for are in the bottom third of the league. You're the team equivalent of that guy who shows up to a potluck with store-bought cookies and somehow wins the dessert contest.

The numbers say you belong lower. The record says you belong higher. The truth is somewhere in the middle — and it's coming for you.

You're not good. You're fortunate. And fortune is a rental.
