## Open questions per keeper

### v8-desktop
- **Mobile story?** Desktop-OS metaphor needs a collapse pattern. Three options worth testing: (1) single-window focus mode, (2) stacked cards that mimic window layering, (3) a mobile-only alternate face that drops the metaphor entirely.
- **Interactivity level?** Is the metaphor just visual, or should windows be draggable / resizable? Draggable is cute, but eats implementation time and doesn't help on mobile.
- **Data density** — desktop metaphor lets us stack multiple "apps" on screen at once. That's either a feature (everything glanceable) or a liability (too much noise). Need to judge against real Week 6+ content.
