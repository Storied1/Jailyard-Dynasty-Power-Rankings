# Concept 01: The Clubhouse

> Generated 2026-04-09 by 10-agent creative team + Creative Director synthesis.
> Status: CANDIDATE (not selected — brainstorming continues)

## Core Identity
The group chat's smarter, meaner, better-dressed older sibling — a living document that takes sides, keeps receipts, and never forgets what you said in Week 3. A private clubhouse for 12 people designed to make every other fantasy league jealous.

## Five Signature Features
1. **Verdict Cards** — Screenshot-ready PNGs per team per week (1200x630). Rank, record, one-line verdict, data portrait. #1 Brag Card, #12 Self-Roast Card.
2. **The Receipt** — Sidebar tracking predictions across the season. Original quote + timestamp + what happened.
3. **Real Game Context** — One sentence per player per week of what happened in the real NFL game.
4. **Franchise Wing** — Permanent biography per team that builds over the season. "You Were There" chat recreations.
5. **Three-Reaction System** — Fire, clown, skull on every blurb. 8+ skulls = "Truth Bomb" tag.

## Design Language
- Near-black (#0a0a0b), warm off-white (#e8e4de), one accent per season (2025: amber #d4a04a)
- Serif headlines (Newsreader) + sans-serif UI (Inter). Drop caps. Pull quotes.
- Subtle grain texture. Hairline card borders. No glassmorphic blur.
- Compact dateline headers. Content-first. Mobile-first.
- Motion: slow, intentional, scroll-driven. Zero infinite animations.

## Season Arc
- Act I: The Overreaction (Wk 1-6) — full format, chaos, possibility
- Act II: The Squeeze (Wk 7-13) — format tightens, Week 10 Bottle Episode
- Act III: The Reckoning (Wk 14-Championship) — stripped, Tale of the Tape, Reunion Special

## Weekly Experience
Cold open (Quote/Stat/Scene) → Essay with real NFL context → Rankings as personal letters with Verdict Cards → Reactions → Callout Board → Predictions

## Shareability Engine
Verdict Cards, bolded kicker lines, "Overheard in the Chat" tagging two people, The Receipt, reaction counts, "Send this to @handle" links.

## History Experience
Championship Gallery (chat quote at moment of victory), Franchise Wing (scrollable biography), Rivalry Collection (dot timeline), Elo Ridgeline Plot (joy plot replacing spaghetti).

## Provocative Elements
- YES: Site-builder tax (Ken-obi roasted 10-15% harder), Callout Board, Prediction Market
- NO: 13th Member, Engagement Walls, Anonymous Confessional, Kill Weekly Column

## What We're NOT Building
No login, no AI banter between members, no participation badges, no chat on site, no "What If" sims, no charts nobody scrolls to, no per-member subscriptions.

## Licensing
Template repo model. "Your league deserves a home page." Fork, edit config.js, deploy. Content engine is the premium layer.
