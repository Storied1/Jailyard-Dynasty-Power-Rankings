2025 Season Preview

The state of the Jailyard on the eve of the 2025 season: the essay, the storylines we’re tracking, and the preseason power rankings, 1 through 12.

The Preview

The Jailyard spent its offseason arguing about tanking. Not one argument — a hundred and twenty-one of them in a single month, plus punishment proposals, rule votes, and at least one poll nobody asked for.

Here’s what the shouting is really about: three seasons, three different champions. The Legion of Bouz in 2022, General Ken-obi in 2023, Kittler on the Roof in 2024. Nobody has repeated, nobody has separated, and every strategy — win-now, tank-forever, hoard-picks — still claims it’s the smart one. 2025 is the referendum.

The favorites look familiar. Ken-obi rolls out four startable quarterbacks and a receiver room with no bad answers. MHJTIME counters with the best wideout trio in the league plus Travis Hunter — and per Sacko, ‘There’s only 1 guy in the history of dynasty fantasy football idp that has ever been designated in sleeper as a DB and a WR.’ We are, apparently, an IDP league. Kittler defends the crown with a starting lineup coming off more rehab appointments than anyone wants to count.

And the receipts are already printing. Sacko, July 29: Skattebo is ‘overated and about to be a bust.’ Karim, three minutes later: ‘Skatt about to go gang busters!’ One of these men will be insufferable by Halloween. Meanwhile Chudders keeps bench-pressing next to the trophy case, two 2026 firsts and two 2027 firsts deep into a rebuild the chat has many opinions about.

Twelve teams, one trophy, and a group chat that forgets nothing. Somebody’s take is going to age like milk in a hot car. This column is where we check the expiration dates.

2025 Preseason Power Rankings

General Ken-obi bLaker24Contender

The consensus No. 1, and it isn’t close. Four startable quarterbacks, Saquon Barkley in his prime, and a CeeDee-Nico-London receiver room deep enough to survive a bye-week apocalypse. The knock? Somebody good has to sit every week. We’re all just hoping you oversleep on waiver day.

#3

Nobody’s starting lineup scares people like yours: Chase, Nabers, Harrison Jr., plus Travis Hunter, the two-way rookie Sacko spent July hyping to anyone who’d listen. Trey McBride is a top-five tight end. Your backfield is Chuba Hubbard and vibes. One real running back and this is your year.

#4

Your receiver room still makes you squint — Worthy is a highlight reel, not an alpha. Pundits in the chat keep saying picks don’t score points. 2025 is where you prove them wrong.

#5

Rasheeing the Scene ToreroGauchoFrisky

Josh Allen is the best quarterback in this league, and your running back committee oozes juice — Achane, Irving, Judkins. Then the receivers show up and the party dies. With Rashee Rice’s suspension looming, you’re one alpha short of dangerous. Weekly thrill ride, as always.

#6

Fraud tier until you pick a direction.

Lamar, Tyreek, and Kelce are still video-game characters, but the age cliff is on the minimap and the pick cupboard is bare. You led the offseason in messages; lead it in wins or start the teardown.
